# Base

The base is SPT's vocabulary: universes and lifting, functions, the empty type and negation, identity types with transport, path induction and their groupoid laws, the unit type, dependent pairs and products, disjoint sums, trichotomy, decidability, the two-point type, the natural numbers with their orders, pairing and elimination, finite types and environments, lists, vectors, partial values, inclusion of predicates, and induction on the arguments of an introduction. Each type former is given once, in its own module, with one notation. The index [Base](../Base.agda) is the one module that is opened whole; every other import names what it uses.

The identity type, Σ, the unit type, the two-point type, ℕ, lists and `Maybe` are the types of Agda's own `Agda.Builtin` modules under SPT's names and fixities, so the standard library shares them. The base imports no module of the standard library and binds no builtin of its own; nothing in it assumes K, and it uses no `rewrite`.

## Index and notation

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Base](../Base.agda) | Re-exports the seventeen modules below. Its header fixes the precedence of every operator of SPT, in the manner of agda-unimath's MIXFIX-OPERATORS.md, lower levels binding more loosely; reserved rows fix the classes of the operators of proof theory and ordinal analysis (judgements, cedents, connectives and atoms of formulas, ordinal sum, product and exponentiation) before their modules exist, so that `Γ ▻ A ∧ B ⊢ C` reads as `(Γ ▻ (A ∧ B)) ⊢ C`. An operator that depends on a structure is a top-level function with the structure in brackets, declared by syntax, as in `x ≺⟨ P ⟩ y`. |

## Types and their elementary laws

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Universes](Universes.agda) | Agda's universes `Set ℓ` and levels (`lzero`, `lsuc`, `_⊔_`), written as Agda writes them with no wrapper of SPT's, and lifting to a higher universe (the record `Lift`, constructor `lift`, field `lower`), which keeps the points and changes only the level. |
| [Functions](Functions.agda) | Dependent functions are Agda's own `(x : A) → B x`; SPT defines no `Π`, which would be a second spelling of that type. The identity function (`id`), composition `g ∘ f`, which is not dependent, and the constant functions (`const`). |
| [Empty](Empty.agda) | The empty type `𝟘`, in the lowest universe, and its elimination into any type (`𝟘-elim`); the negation `¬ A`, the maps from `A` into `𝟘`. |
| [Identity](Identity.agda) | The identity type `x ＝ y`, generated by `refl`: the identity type of `Agda.Builtin.Equality` under SPT's name and fixity, so also the standard library's `_≡_`. An identification is matched against `refl` only where its endpoints are free to be unified, and path induction computes at `refl` (`identity-elim`). Application (`ap`, `ap₂`, and `apd` for dependent functions), transport along a family (`transport`, `transport₂`), composition `p ∙ q` and inversion `p ⁻¹`, associative with `refl` as unit and `p ⁻¹` as inverse up to identification (`refl-left-neutral`, `refl-right-neutral`, `left-inverse`, `right-inverse`). The laws of `ap` (`ap-id`, `ap-∙`, `ap-⁻¹`, `ap-∘`, `ap-const`) and of transport along composites, applications and constant families, back along inverses (`transport-∙`, `transport-ap`, `transport-const`, `forth-and-back-transport`, `back-and-forth-transport`); transport is injective (`transport-is-injective`), commutes with maps of fibers (`fiberwise-maps-commute-with-transport`), composes in families of identifications (`transport-fiber`, `transport-is-post-composition`) and carries predicates on fibers (`dependent-transport`). Cancellation and transposition of composites (`inverse-of-composite`, `right-factor-cancels`, `middle-factor-cancels`, `right-cancellable`, `left-transpose`, `trivial-difference`), rearrangements of pasting equations (`cancel-ap`, `inverse-ap`, `move-end`, `move-unit`, `pentagonal`), a path through a retraction (`path-around-retraction`), and the naturality of homotopies (`homotopies-are-natural`, `identity-homotopies-are-natural`). Apartness `x ≠ y`, and chains `x ＝⟨ p ⟩ y ＝⟨ q ⟩ z ∎`. |
| [Unit](Unit.agda) | The unit type `𝟙` with its one point `⋆`, the record of `Agda.Builtin.Unit` with no fields, so every point is `⋆` by η, definitionally (`every-point-is-⋆`). |
| [Sigma](Sigma.agda) | Dependent pairs `Σ A (λ x → B x)`, the record of `Agda.Builtin.Sigma` with projections `pr₁` and `pr₂`, written prefix and built with `_,_`, so also the standard library's Σ; products `A × B`, the pairs of a constant family, never written as Σ. Two pairs are identified by an identification of their first components and one of their second components over it (`to-Σ-＝`), which projects back to the first (`ap-pr₁-to-Σ-＝`); every identification of pairs carries the second components along the first (`from-Σ-＝-pr₂`). Transport of the second component of a section (`transport-section-pr₂`), and transport through a family of retractions over a map of bases (`section-along-retraction`, `pair-along-retraction`). |
| [Sum](Sum.agda) | Disjoint sums `A ⊎ B` (`inl`, `inr`), maps out of them by cases (`cases`), sections of families over them (`⊎-induction`) and maps of sums keeping the side (`⊎-map`); the injections never meet and are injective (`inl-is-not-inr`, `inl-is-injective`, `inr-is-injective`). The sum is written `⊎`, keeping `+` for addition of numbers and of ordinals. |
| [Trichotomy](Trichotomy.agda) | Trichotomy of two points for a strict relation `<` up to a relation `≈` (`Trichotomy _≈_ _<_ x y`): `x < y`, `x ≈ y` or `y < x`, the relations given first so that they fix the type of the points. |
| [Decidable](Decidable.agda) | Decidable types and types with decidable equality (`is-decidable`, `has-decidable-equality`), the decision a point of a disjoint sum, retained, not a mere statement that one side holds. |
| [Two](Two.agda) | The two-point type `𝟚`, the type of `Agda.Builtin.Bool` with `₀` for `false` and `₁` for `true`; its points are distinct (`₀-is-not-₁`) and its equality is decidable (`𝟚-has-decidable-equality`). Its dependent elimination (`two-elim`) and the conditional `𝟚-cases x y`, sending `₀` to `x` and `₁` to `y`. |
| [Natural](Natural.agda) | The natural numbers of `Agda.Builtin.Nat`, with addition `+`, multiplication `*` and truncated subtraction `∸` evaluated by Agda's primitives, and numerals read as points of ℕ; dependent elimination (`natural-elim`); the laws of addition at zero and at successors on the right, and its associativity (`+-zero-right`, `+-suc-right`, `+-assoc`). The structural order `m ≤ℕˢ n`, generated by `zero ≤ℕˢ n` and its successor rule, and `m <ℕˢ n`, the order to induct along, with reflexivity, transitivity and trichotomy (`≤ℕˢ-refl`, `≤ℕˢ-trans`, `<ℕˢ-trichotomy`), and small facts of it (`≤ℕˢ-to-suc`, `nothing-below-zero`, `below-one-is-zero`). The maximum `m ⊔ℕ n`, an upper bound of its arguments, idempotent, and the larger of two comparable numbers (`≤ℕˢ-⊔ˡ`, `≤ℕˢ-⊔ʳ`, `⊔ℕ-idempotent`, `≤ℕˢ-gives-⊔ℕ-right`, `≤ℕˢ-gives-⊔ℕ-left`). The stepwise order `m ≤ℕ′ n`, which recurses on the target, given by every structural inequality (`≤ℕˢ-to-≤ℕ′`), along which a sequence rising at every step rises (`increasing-along-≤ℕ′`, `increasing-from-steps`). `zero` is not a successor, the successor is injective, and equality is decidable (`zero-is-not-suc`, `suc-is-injective`, `ℕ-has-decidable-equality`). An injective pairing of `ℕ × ℕ` into ℕ along the diagonals, with unpairing as a left inverse (`ℕ-pair`, `ℕ-unpair`, `ℕ-unpair-pair`). The computational order is in [Foundation/Nat/Order](../Foundation/Nat/Order.agda). |
| [Fin](Fin.agda) | `Fin n`, the `n` numbers below `n` (`fzero`, `fsuc`), with the number of each point (`to-ℕ`), an injective successor and decidable equality (`fzero-is-not-fsuc`, `fsuc-is-injective`, `Fin-has-decidable-equality`); numerals `# m`, whose bound `is-below m n` is the unit or the empty type and is found by computation; the embeddings of `Fin m` and `Fin n` in `Fin (m + n)`, at the same number (`i ↑ˡ n`) and after the first `m` numbers (`m ↑ʳ j`). `Fin (m + n)` is the sum of `Fin m` and `Fin n`: splitting and joining are inverse (`split-at`, `join-at`, `join-at-split-at`, `split-at-join-at`), and the numbers of embedded points are their own and moved by `m` (`to-ℕ-↑ˡ`, `to-ℕ-↑ʳ`); every number is below its bound and determines its point (`to-ℕ-is-below`, `to-ℕ-is-injective`). Environments `Fin n → A`, extended by a point at the newest position (`extend-environment`) and, given a default point, to all numbers (`extend-to-naturals`, `extended-related-at`). |
| [List](List.agda) | The lists of `Agda.Builtin.List` (`[]`, `x ∷ xs`), concatenation `xs ++ ys`, associative with `[]` as unit, and length, which adds under concatenation (`length-++`); lists under a map (`list-map`); a predicate at every member (`List-All`, whose constructors `[]` and `_∷_` share the names of the list constructors, carried along maps of predicates by `all-map`) and at some member (`List-Any`, with `at-head` and `in-tail`); membership `x ∈ˡ xs`, the positions of `x` in `xs`; the injectivity of `_∷_` (`∷-injective-head`, `∷-injective-tail`); and the entry at a position below the length (`list-lookup`). |
| [Maybe](Maybe.agda) | Partial values, the `Maybe A` of `Agda.Builtin.Maybe` (`nothing`, `just a`): the two cases never meet and `just` is injective (`nothing-is-not-just`, `just-is-injective`); being just and being nothing are types with at most one point, decided by inspecting the value, never both (`is-just`, `is-nothing`, `being-just-is-decidable`, `not-both-just-and-nothing`), and a value that is just has its point (`from-just`). Partial values under a map (`maybe-map`), and read with a default (`maybe-rec`). |
| [Vector](Vector.agda) | Lists of a given length, `Vec A n`, whose constructors `[]` and `_∷_` share the names and fixity of the list constructors and are told apart by type, and the entry at a position (`vector-lookup`). |
| [Predicate](Predicate.agda) | Inclusion `X ⊆ᵖ Y` and equivalence `X ≈ᵖ Y` of type-valued predicates on one type: a map of witnesses at every point, and maps both ways with no inverse law (`≈ᵖ-sym`). |
| [Induction](Induction.agda) | Induction on the arguments of an introduction `make : A → C` whose arguments have inputs at positions: steps, induction and its computation identity (`Argument-Step`, `Argument-Induction`, `Argument-Computation`). Induction into the level of the arguments presents every element as `make` of an argument (`argument-presentation`, `argument-root`, `make-argument-root`), and with the computation identity the root inverts `make`, which is injective (`argument-root-make`, `make-injective`). The source arguments of a Dybjer–Setzer description are an instance ([IR/Universal/Source](../IR/Universal/Source.agda)); initiality, under extensionality, is in [Foundation/Induction/Initiality](../Foundation/Induction/Initiality.agda). |
