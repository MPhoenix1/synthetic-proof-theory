# Incidence

These are the modules of SPT's incidence calculus that the release uses: boundary spaces over an ambient displayed space ([Foundation/Spaces](../Foundation/Spaces.agda)), with their base change and postcomposition, and the graph of a map decorated by a family, compared with the pullback of that family.

## Boundary spaces and graphs

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Boundary/Base](Boundary/Base.agda) | Boundary spaces over a fixed ambient displayed space U (the record `∂-Space`): a displayed space X of boundaries and fillers with a total map realizing each filled cell as a complete point of U, `realization : Π (b : Base X). Fiber X b → Σ (a : Base U). Fiber U a`. Boundaries, fillers and filled cells are the base, the fibers and the total points of `space X`, with no second names. A split realization fixes the ambient base from the boundary before the filler is read (`Is-Split`; `present-split` is split by `present-split-is-split`). `𝒰 ℓ` is the displayed space of small types and one inhabitant. Base change along `q : B → Base U` is the pullback of the fibers of U along q (`base-change`), and postcomposition with a total map from U to V changes only the realized point (`postcompose`). A family R over `Total U` is the split boundary space whose boundaries are the complete ambient points and whose realization forgets the filler (`family-boundary-space`); fiberwise isomorphisms `R u ≅ Q u` give an isomorphism of filled cells that keeps the boundary and commutes with realization by refl (`family-filled-cell-isomorphism`). No section, reconstruction, uniqueness or generated incidence is asserted. |
| [Displayed/Graph](Displayed/Graph.agda) | The graph of a map `output : I → D`, decorated by a family C over D, against the pullback of C along `output`: `Graph-Occurrence C output = Σ (d : D). Σ (i : I). (output i ＝ d) × C d`, and the total of `pullback-space C output` is `Σ (i : I). C (output i)`. An occurrence keeps its output, its index, their identification and its cell; path induction recovers the complete occurrence from its index and cell (`recover-occurrence`), so occurrences and the pullback total are isomorphic (`graph-occurrence-isomorphism`). A family Q over occurrences moves to the pullback: its dependent pairs exactly (`graph-family-total-isomorphism`), its sections pointwise in both directions (`descend-sections`, `ascend-sections`), and as an isomorphism of the two section types under extensionality for both, passed explicitly (`graph-family-sections-isomorphism`). |
