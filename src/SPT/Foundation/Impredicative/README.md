# Impredicative principles

This folder holds impredicative principles of the foundation, each in a module of its own, with the comparisons between them, obstructions to their exact forms, and their theorems. A principle is a type, never assumed: its module states it and proves what follows from an inhabitant.

Two spellings are fixed throughout. Weak resizing is the resizing of [Classification](Classification.agda) at the family of all types of one level, written `Resizing (λ (X : Set ℓ) → X)`, and the classifier of a level is `Classifier (λ (X : Set ℓ) → X)`; neither has a definition of its own. Inclusion and equivalence of predicates are those of [Base/Predicate](../../Base/Predicate.agda) (`_⊆ᵖ_`, `_≈ᵖ_`).

## Principles

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Classification](Classification.agda) | Logical classification of one family of types by another: every member of the first has maps both ways with a member of the second, and no inverse laws are asked (`Classifies F G`). For a Tarski family (U , T), resizing, every type of the level of U classified by T (`Resizing T`), and a classifier, a code `proxies` with a family `proxy` of codes over its decoding such that T ∘ proxy classifies every decoding of T (`Classifier T`; `chosen`, `encode`, `decode`). |
| [WeakResizing](WeakResizing.agda) | Weak presentations, a type of a level with maps to the given type and back and no inverse law (`Weak-Presentation`); weak resizing, `Resizing (λ (X : Set ℓ) → X)`, gives every type of the next level a weak presentation, and conversely (`weak-resizing-presents`, `presentations-give-weak-resizing`). Under double negation every type is presented by the unit or the empty type (`every-type-is-presented-under-double-negation`), so that statement alone has no content. |
| [PropositionalResizing](PropositionalResizing.agda) | A proposition with a weak presentation at a level is small at that level, as the fixed points of a weakly constant endomap of the presenting type (`weakly-presented-propositions-are-small`). Propositional resizing, every proposition of the next level small (`Propositional-Resizing`), which weak resizing gives (`weak-resizing-gives-propositional-resizing`); under it a cover at ℓ is a cover at ℓ + 1 (`covers-rise-under-resizing`). Resizing between any two levels (`Resized`, `Propositional-Resizing-Between`), every proposition resizing to its own level (`propositions-resize-to-their-level`); from ℓ + 1 to ℓ the two forms are one strength (`propositional-resizing-between-levels`, `resizing-between-levels-gives-propositional-resizing`). |
| [PropositionalClassifier](PropositionalClassifier.agda) | Classifiers of propositions, a small family in which every small proposition has a logically equivalent member, with no inverse law (`Propositional-Classifier`); a classifier has distinct true and false members (`proxies-are-distinct`) and gives each small type a small type of characteristic maps (`Small-Power`, `characteristic-is-complete`). Excluded middle gives one with proxies in 𝟚 ([ExcludedMiddle](../ExcludedMiddle.agda)'s `classifier-from-excluded-middle`). |
| [Classifier](Classifier.agda) | The classifier of a level, `Classifier (λ (X : Set ℓ) → X)`: a small family of proxies with maps both ways between every small type and one proxy, the recovery logical only. Restricted to propositions it is a classifier of propositions (`classifier-gives-propositional-classifier`). Power presentations, a small type of subsets retaining every small predicate up to pointwise logical equivalence (`Power-Presentation`): a classifier gives every small type one, the choices of a proxy at each point (`classifier-gives-power-presentations`), and the power presentation of the unit type is a classifier (`power-presentation-of-the-unit-gives-classifier`), so powers are exactly a classifier. With weak resizing the proxies classify the types of the next level too (`raised-types-are-classified`). |
| [SmallIdentifications](SmallIdentifications.agda) | Small identifications, every identification of two types of level ℓ + 1 small at ℓ (`Small-Identifications`). No impredicativity at ℓ is assumed, yet under univalence at ℓ + 1 they give propositional resizing, a proposition P being equivalent to P ＝ 1 (`small-identifications-give-resizing`), and with function extensionality at ℓ + 1 a type represented faithfully by automorphisms of a set of level ℓ + 1 is small (`faithfully-represented-types-are-small`). With propositional extensionality ([Propositions/Extensionality](../Propositions/Extensionality.agda)) and function extensionality at ℓ, every ¬¬-separated set of level ℓ + 1 is so represented, toggling the ¬¬-stable proposition beside each x by x ＝ a ([Modalities/Biconditional](../Modalities/Biconditional.agda)), so it is small (`separated-sets-are-small`). So a large ¬¬-separated set refutes small identifications (`large-separated-sets-refute-small-identifications`); that small identifications give one is the specification `Separated-Largeness`, inhabited in [BuraliForti](BuraliForti.agda). |

## Obstructions

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Classifier/Discriminator](Classifier/Discriminator.agda) | Recovery is the content of a power presentation: the power type of every small type has a lawless small weak presentation, by the unit type with the empty predicate as its return (`lawless-presentation`), whose maps admit no recovery law at an inhabited type (`lawless-presentation-is-unrecoverable`). So a weak presentation of the power type is no power presentation, and the `recover` field of `Power-Presentation` carries the strength. |
| [BuraliForti](BuraliForti.agda) | Burali-Forti for stably well-founded orders ([WellFounded/Stable](../WellFounded/Stable.agda)), read through double negation. Under function extensionality at ℓ and ℓ + 1, propositional extensionality at ℓ and propositional resizing (the record `Hypotheses`), the order types of the orders of level ℓ, the ¬¬-stable predicates on orders that are doubly not the class of an order under ¬¬-isomorphism (`Order-Types`), form a ¬¬-separated set; ordered by being, doubly not, isomorphic to segments of one another, they are stably well-founded (`order-types-are-stably-well-founded`), and they are not small (`order-types-are-not-small`), since a small presentation of them is an order whose segments have types increasing strictly and below its own. So under propositional extensionality at ℓ and univalence at ℓ + 1, small identifications give a large separated set (`separated-largeness`) and are contradictory (`small-identifications-are-contradictory`), with no impredicativity assumed and no excluded middle. |
| [SmallIdentifications/Cayley](SmallIdentifications/Cayley.agda) | Cayley's embedding under small identifications: a group of level ℓ + 1 ([Groups](../Groups.agda)) acts faithfully on its elements by left multiplication, so under univalence at ℓ + 1 its elements form a small type (`elements-of-groups-are-small`). Transporting the multiplication along that equivalence, which the module does not do, gives an isomorphic group of level ℓ; so a group of level ℓ + 1 isomorphic to no group of level ℓ, such as the free group on the ordinals of level ℓ (Bezem, Coquand, Dybjer and Escardó; TypeTopology's Ordinals.BuraliForti, `there-is-a-large-group`), refutes small identifications. |

## Theorems

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [PropositionalResizing/Truncation](PropositionalResizing/Truncation.agda) | Propositional truncations from propositional resizing, after Voevodsky: the cover `Covered ℓ X` of a type of level ℓ is a proposition of level ℓ + 1 under function extensionality at that level (`covers-are-propositions`), and under the same extensionality propositional resizing presents it at level ℓ as a truncation at ℓ (`truncation-from-resizing`). One level up, under the same hypotheses, the cover itself is a truncation, with no resizing of the cover: resizing lowers instead the proposition it eliminates into (`truncation-one-level-up`). |
| [PropositionalClassifier/Exact](PropositionalClassifier/Exact.agda) | Exact classifiers of propositions, a small family of propositions in which every small proposition is isomorphic to a member (`Exact-Classifier`), obtained from a classifier up to logical equivalence (`exact-classifier`): a member is a proxy with a weakly constant endomap of its points (`Constant-Proxy`), and is the type of fixed points of that endomap (`fixed-member`), so a proposition. The exact index retains the proxy literally (`exact-index-retains-the-proxy`), forgetting the inverse laws gives a classifier up to logical equivalence (`exact-classifier-is-propositional`), and with propositional resizing the propositions of the next universe are classified as well (`raised-propositions-are-classified`). Under function extensionality the family is closed under products over every small type, its own type of proxies included (`exact-product`), so it carries the rules of Rathjen's impredicative universe of propositions of MLV_P (Rathjen 2012, §4; `Proposition-Universe`, `universe-of-exact-classifier`); where MLV_P asks proof irrelevance and T_P(0_P) = N₀ judgmentally, here they are a proof that proofs are propositions and an elimination into the empty type. |
| [Tripos](Tripos.agda), [Tripos/Indexed](Tripos/Indexed.agda) | The canonical hyperdoctrine of small predicates: over a base of types of level α, the predicates on X are the maps `X → Set ℓ`, ordered by inclusion and reindexed by precomposition, with the pointwise Heyting operations and their universal properties, which reindexing preserves by computation (`reindex`, `reindex-preserves-the-heyting-operations`). A tripos adds quantifiers along every map of the base, adjoint to reindexing and satisfying Beck–Chevalley along the canonical pullbacks (`Canonical-Pullback`, `Quantifiers`), and a generic predicate (`Generic-Predicate`, `Tripos`). Over the small base the quantifiers are Σ and Π along the fibers with no hypothesis (`canonical-quantifiers`), and a generic predicate is exactly a classifier, so the hyperdoctrine is a tripos exactly when there is a classifier (`classifier-gives-tripos`, `tripos-gives-classifier`); its power objects classify relations and are power presentations (`power-objects-classify-relations`, `generic-predicate-gives-power-presentations`). Over the base of the next level the generic predicate is free (`types-are-generic`) and weak resizing supplies the quantifiers (`weak-resizing-gives-quantifiers`, `weak-resizing-gives-tripos`); quantifiers over the next level give each of its types a small reflection (`quantifiers-give-small-reflections`), and no converse to weak resizing is claimed. Indexed triposes, with predicates of any form and entailments at independent levels (`Indexed-Tripos`): a tripos of small predicates is one, with every reindexing law by computation (`tripos-gives-indexed-tripos`), and the realizability tripos is another. |
| [Realizability/CombinatoryAlgebra](Realizability/CombinatoryAlgebra.agda), [Realizability/Recursion](Realizability/Recursion.agda), [Realizability/Two](Realizability/Two.agda) | Partial combinatory algebras, a functional partial application given by its graph, with combinators k and s and their laws (`Partial-Combinatory-Algebra`); terms in n variables evaluate partially in an environment (`Term`, `Evaluates`), and bracket abstraction is always defined and applies as the abstracted term (`abstraction-defined`, `abstraction-β`), which gives the combinators realizers are built from (`identity`, `composite`, `pairing`, `injection`, `copairing`, `curry`, `uncurry`, `constant-after`). Recursion in every partial combinatory algebra, with no further constructor: Turing's fixed point guarded by an abstraction (`fix-β`), pairs and conditionals, numerals (`Numeral`), and recursors that unfold only the branch their input selects, so an unused recursive call is never evaluated (`natural-recursor`, `tree-recursor`). Realizers of the two points in an algebra at level zero (`Bit`). |
| [Realizability/Tripos](Realizability/Tripos.agda) | The realizability tripos of a partial combinatory algebra over the base of small types: a predicate on X assigns to each point a subset of the carrier, a point of a presentation of the subsets (`Subset-Presentation`), and φ entails ψ when one element sends every realizer of φ x to a realizer of ψ x, uniformly in x (`Entailment`). The operations and their universal properties need no smallness of the subsets, every predicate being its own subset one level up (`predicates-are-subsets`); only the generic predicate needs a power presentation (`realizability-tripos`), which a classifier supplies (`classifier-gives-realizability-tripos`). At the one-point algebra entailment is inclusion at the point (`one-point-entailment-is-inclusion`), and the generic predicate read at the point is a classifier (`one-point-tripos-gives-classifier`). |
