# Foundation

SPT's elementary type theory, written on [the base](../Base.agda) alone: identifications and transport; isomorphisms, their half-adjoint coherence, and equivalences; contractible types, propositions and sets; function extensionality and univalence; fibers and displayed spaces; covers, truncations and modalities; sequential limits; elementary types and structures; natural numbers; well-foundedness and induction; signatures with their translations and polynomials; trees with their folds and initiality; binding signatures, with renaming and substitution; and impredicative principles. Each module is imported by name where it is used.

Function extensionality is a hypothesis for one dependent function type at a time, `FunExt A B`, passed explicitly to the theorems that use it; univalence, propositional truncation, excluded middle and every impredicative principle are hypotheses in the same way, never assumed. Every module is `--safe` and `--without-K`, and none uses `--two-level`: strict identification and the other results of two-level type theory are in [TwoLevel](../TwoLevel/README.md).

## Isomorphisms, half-adjoint data and equivalences

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Isomorphisms](Isomorphisms.agda) | Isomorphisms `A ≅ B`, the record of two maps with pointwise inverse laws, with identity, inverse and composite (`≅-refl`, `≅-sym`, `≅-trans`); isomorphisms of dependent pairs over one index (`Σ-cong`), reassociation (`Σ-assoc`), pairs over the point (`Σ-over-𝟙`), identifications of pairs as pairs of identifications (`Σ-＝-≅`), and transport as an isomorphism of fibers (`transport-is-isomorphism`); dependent functions on pointwise isomorphisms, under extensionality for both function types (`Π-cong`). |
| [Isomorphisms/Sets](Isomorphisms/Sets.agda) | An identification is recovered from its backward image (`recovered-from-backward-image`), so a type isomorphic to a set is a set (`isomorphic-to-a-set`). |
| [HalfAdjoint](HalfAdjoint.agda) | Every isomorphism carries the coherence of a half-adjoint equivalence, with no further hypothesis (`coherent-forward-backward`, `half-adjoint-condition`). So an isomorphism acts on identifications by an isomorphism (`ap-is-isomorphism`), dependent pairs over an isomorphism of indices are isomorphic (`Σ-bicong`), and so are dependent functions, under extensionality for both function types (`Π-bicong`). As in TypeTopology's UF.Equiv and the HoTT book, Theorem 2.11.1. |
| [HalfAdjoint/Extension](HalfAdjoint/Extension.agda) | A section given on the image of an isomorphism extends along the coherent inverse law to the whole codomain (`extension`), is the given section on the image (`extension-β`), and keeps every predicate it satisfies at the images (`extension-preserves-predicates`). |
| [Equivalences](Equivalences.agda) | Under function extensionality, named for the function types it is needed for: being contractible and being an equivalence are propositions (`being-contractible-is-proposition`, `being-equivalence-is-proposition`), and two equivalences with one map are identified (`equivalences-with-one-map`); under extensionality at one level, the equivalences into a set form a set (`equivalences-into-a-set-form-a-set`). |
| [Embeddings](Embeddings.agda) | Maps whose fibers are propositions (`is-embedding`), a proposition under function extensionality (`being-an-embedding-is-a-proposition`). Maps with contractible fibers, lowering out of a lift, first projections of families of propositions and composites of embeddings are embeddings; embeddings are left-cancellable, and left-cancellable maps into sets are embeddings (`embeddings-are-left-cancellable`, `left-cancellable-maps-into-sets-are-embeddings`). |
| [Lifting](Lifting.agda) | A type is isomorphic to its lift, isomorphic types have isomorphic lifts, and lifting and lowering of identifications are inverse (`lifting-is-isomorphism`, `lifts-of-isomorphic-types`, `lower-of-lift`, `lift-of-lower`). |
| [LogicalEquivalence](LogicalEquivalence.agda) | Logical equivalence `A ⇔ B = (A → B) × (B → A)`: reflexive, symmetric and transitive, given by identification (`⇔-of-＝`), and a congruence for products, sums and function types, decidability, Π and Σ over one index, dependent functions guarded by a family along an isomorphism of indices, and a propositional modality (`×-iff`, `⊎-iff`, `→-iff`, `decidable-iff`, `Π-iff`, `Σ-iff`, `guard-iff`, `○-iff`). |

## Contractible types, propositions and sets

| Module | Contents |

| [Propositions/Closure](Propositions/Closure.agda), [Propositions/Extensionality](Propositions/Extensionality.agda) | A type isomorphic to a proposition is one (`isomorphic-to-a-proposition`), and the fixed points of a weakly constant endomap form a proposition (`fixed-points-form-a-proposition`, TypeTopology's `fix-is-prop`); propositional extensionality at a level (`Propositional-Extensionality`), which univalence at the level gives (`univalence-gives-propositional-extensionality`). |
| [Sets](Sets.agda) | Retracts of propositions are propositions (`retracts-of-propositions-are-propositions`); under extensionality for the dependent functions of one level, being a proposition and being a set are propositions, and products of sets are sets (`being-a-proposition-is-a-proposition`, `being-a-set-is-a-proposition`, `products-of-sets-are-sets`); sums of sets over a set, lifts of sets, and sums of 1-types over a 1-type (`sums-of-sets-are-sets`, `lifts-of-sets-are-sets`, `sums-of-1-types-are-1-types`). |
| [Sets/Uniqueness](Sets/Uniqueness.agda) | Unique identifications from one point (`Unique`, `identifications-from`), passing through retractions, transport and dependent pairs (`retract-unique`, `transported-unique`, `pair-unique`); under function extensionality at every level, the record `Extensionality`, through functions, so dependent products of sets are sets (`function-unique`, `Π-is-set`). `UIP`, every type at every level a set, is stated and not assumed. |
| [Hedberg](Hedberg.agda) | Hedberg's theorem: a type with decidable equality is a set (`decidable-equality-gives-set`); ℕ and 𝟚 are sets (`ℕ-is-set`, `𝟚-is-set`). |
| [Size](Size.agda) | Smallness at a level, `is-small ℓ X = Σ (K : Set ℓ). Equivalence K X`, as in TypeTopology's UF.Size; a proposition under univalence at that level and function extensionality (`being-small-is-proposition`). |

## Function extensionality and univalence

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Extensionality](Extensionality.agda), [Extensionality/Levels](Extensionality/Levels.agda) | Function extensionality for one dependent function type, the hypothesis `FunExt A B`; under it, a dependent function type of propositions is a proposition (`Π-is-proposition`). Extensionality at one level gives it at every function type below that level (`funext-below`). |
| [Happly](Happly.agda) | Homotopies from identifications of functions (`happly`), with its laws for composites, inverses, pre- and post-composition and transport (`happly-∙`, `happly-⁻¹`, `happly-pre`, `happly-post`, `transport-at`). The homotopies from a function form a contractible type (`homotopies-are-contractible`), which gives homotopy induction (`homotopy-induction`); under extensionality, `happly` has an inverse adjusted to send the constant homotopy to refl (`funext`, `funext-refl`, `funext-happly`, `happly-funext`), and a loop of functions that is refl at every point is refl (`pointwise-trivial-loops-are-refl`). As in TypeTopology's UF.Base, by homotopy induction. |
| [Happly/Pointwise](Happly/Pointwise.agda) | The adjusted extensionality at every level, from function extensionality at every level (`Extensionality`), sealed (`pointwise`), with its value at each argument (`pointwise-at`) and its unfolding as an equation (`pointwise-equation`). |
| [Univalence](Univalence.agda) | Univalence as a hypothesis, at one pair of types, at one level, and at every level (`is-univalent-at`, `is-univalent`, `Univalence`). Equivalences are maps with contractible fibers (`Equivalence`); isomorphisms are equivalences through their half-adjoint coherence, and conversely (`isomorphism-to-equivalence`, `equivalence-to-isomorphism`); `idtoeq`, `isotoid`, and transport along it (`transport-isotoid`); families with an isomorphism at every point are identified, under extensionality for families and univalence at each pair of fibers (`pointwise-isotoid`). Univalence gives function extensionality, by Voevodsky's argument (`funext-into`, `funext-from-univalence`, `univalence-gives-funext`). As in TypeTopology's UF.Univalence and UF.UA-FunExt. |
| [Univalence/Sets](Univalence/Sets.agda) | Under univalence at two types, the second a set, and extensionality at their level, their identifications form a set (`identifications-with-a-set-form-a-set`); so two sets at which univalence holds have a set of identifications, and the type of sets is a 1-type (`universe-of-sets-is-a-1-type`). |

## Covers, truncations and modalities

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Covers](Covers.agda) | Existence observed by the propositions of one level, predicatively and with no truncation: `Covered κ A = Π (P : Set κ). is-proposition P → (A → P) → P`. A point covers its type, covers move along maps and compose, a covered proposition has a point, and a covered type is not refuted; being covered is a proposition under function extensionality for the three function types in its definition (`being-covered-is-proposition`). Covers, maps whose fibers are covered (`Cover`), with descent along them (`descend`); split covers are covers (`Split-Cover`, `split-covers-are-covers`), and a cover with propositional fibers splits; identities, composites, pullbacks and maps pointwise equal to covers are covers. |
| [Covers/WeaklyInitial](Covers/WeaklyInitial.agda) | Weakly initial covers of a type in section form (the record `Weakly-Initial-Covers`), every cover being refined by one of their projections (`refinements`); weakly initial covers by sets at a set (`Weakly-Initial-Set-Covers`, the WISC of Fiore, Pitts and Steenkamp, LMCS 2022, §4), and global choices of either (`Global-Weakly-Initial-Set-Covers`, `Global-Weakly-Initial-Covers`). At a type with choice (`Projective`) the identity cover alone is weakly initial (`projective-types-have-weakly-initial-covers`), so choice for the small sets gives global weakly initial covers by sets (`choice-gives-weakly-initial-set-covers`); isomorphisms and first projections of families of covered types are covers (`isomorphisms-are-covers`, `first-projections-cover`). |
| [Truncations](Truncations.agda) | Propositional truncation at one level as a hypothesis, the record `Truncation ℓ` (`∥ X ∥`, `∣_∣`, `∥∥-rec`), never assumed; it is functorial (`∥∥-map`, `∥∥-bind`). |
| [Modalities](Modalities.agda) | Propositional modalities at one level (the record `Modality`): monads valued in propositions under which the empty type stays empty (`dense`), lying between the identity and double negation (`○-implies-¬¬`); stable types (`is-stable`), closed under products and dependent functions, and stable propositions (`Stable-Proposition`). Instances: truncation (`truncation-modality`), and double negation, under function extensionality for the function types `¬ X → 𝟘` (`double-negation-modality`). |
| [Modalities/Biconditional](Modalities/Biconditional.agda) | The biconditional of ¬¬-stable propositions (`biconditional`), under which toggling by Q undoes itself up to maps both ways (`toggled-twice`, `untoggled`); under propositional extensionality at the level, toggling is an involution and the ¬¬-stable propositions form a set (`toggling-is-an-involution`, `stable-propositions-form-a-set`). |
| [ExcludedMiddle](ExcludedMiddle.agda) | Excluded middle for the propositions of one level, as a hypothesis (`Excluded-Middle`). It descends a level (`excluded-middle-descends`) and makes decided propositions small (`decided-propositions-are-small`), so at ℓ + 1 it gives propositional resizing at ℓ (`excluded-middle-gives-resizing`), and with function extensionality at ℓ + 1 a truncation at ℓ (`excluded-middle-gives-truncation`); at one level, the two truth values indexed by 𝟚 classify the propositions (`classifier-from-excluded-middle`). |

## Binding signatures

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Tree/Binding](Tree/Binding.agda) | Binding signatures, operation names with their premises and the number of variables each premise binds (`Binding`), as signatures over binder depths: a premise binding e variables of an operation at depth d sits at depth e + d (`over`). At depth d under an outer scope of n variables the variables are `Fin (d + n)` (`Variable`), and the expressions in scope n are the open trees at depth 0 with those variables as holes (`Expression`). Renaming is `map`, lifted at each depth (`lift`, `rename`); moving an expression under d binders is `map-lax-tree` along the shift of depths by d, whose boundary coherence is associativity of addition (`shift`, `moved`, `under`); the same translation reads an expression of scope d + n as the premise of a binder of depth d, its first d variables bound (`rebound`, `bound`); substitution is `bind`, bound variables staying and what it substitutes moving under the binders above each variable (`replacement`, `substitute`). | [Tree/Binding/Shift](Tree/Binding/Shift.agda), [Tree/Binding/Transport](Tree/Binding/Transport.agda), [Tree/Binding/Moving](Tree/Binding/Moving.agda) | The arithmetic of moving variables under binders: at depth c, moving a variable under d new binders fixes the c bound variables and adds d to the others (`shifted`, `moved-bound`, `moved-free`), and shifting by e and then by d is shifting by e + d (`shifted-compose`, `moved-compose`). Along an identification of depths, transport of a tree passes through holes and nodes (`transport-hole`, `transport-node`); transport along judgments for every signature is Tree/LaxTranslation/Functoriality's. | [Tree/Binding/Laws](Tree/Binding/Laws.agda) | The laws of renaming and substitution, specialized from those of `map` and `bind`: for any signature a map is the bind of its holes (`map-bind-rel`); lifting the identity and a composite (`lift-identity`, `lift-compose`), so renaming is functorial (`rename-identity-rel`, `rename-compose-rel`); substituting variables is the identity (`substitute-identity-rel`), and renaming is substitution of variables (`rename-substitute-rel`).
