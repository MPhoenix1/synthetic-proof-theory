# Drafts of paper proofs

These notes are drafts of proofs on paper. They are not checked in Agda,
except for the steps that each note says are checked. Each note states its
status in the line under its title. The notes also mention, without links,
material that is not included in this release: LCTT, the note Model.md,
and the cubical Agda modules in which some steps are checked.

| Note | Contents | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The ambient Mahlo rule in simplicial sets (UIR7)](Simplicial.md) | Claims an element of `Mahlo (Ambient.formers ℓ)`, alongside univalence, in Kapulkin–Lumsdaine's simplicial model at a strongly Mahlo cardinal κ. On each path component of the operators the carrier is the universe at the least inaccessible closure point of the component's bound, and `inner` with `inner-β` is a section of one naming fibration, by Christensen's vertex detection. Through Mahlo/DS, the forms of induction–recursion derived there, with decoding as an identity, would then be consistent with univalence relative to ZFC with a Mahlo cardinal and inaccessibles above it. Modules: [Mahlo/Specification](../Mahlo/Specification.agda), [Mahlo/Ambient](../Mahlo/Ambient.agda), [Mahlo/DS](../Mahlo/DS/README.md), [Mahlo/DS/Ambient](../Mahlo/DS/Ambient.agda), [IR/Smallness](../IR/Smallness.agda); the core theory, [MartinLof](../MartinLof/README.md). | Sketch, revised 2026-11-01 after an outside review, which judged the construction viable. Next: a detailed proof. |
| [The strict ambient Mahlo rule in simplicial sets](StrictMahlo.md) | For a graded universe at a strongly Mahlo κ, one chain over the whole space of operators gives every operator a child code with its interpretation, formers and `inner`, each decoding law an equality of maps (Theorem A). Mahlo is used once, to stop each operator simplex's chain below κ, which makes the carrier κ-small; the closure is the initial algebra of its step functor. Read in two-level type theory this is a global element of `Strict-Mahlo (Ambient.formers ℓ)` (Corollary B), and, with a strongly Mahlo cardinal for the level of each stratum and an inaccessible above them, under the soundness assumption T2, LCTT's syntax with every rule has no closed term of empty (Corollary C). Modules: [TwoLevel/Mahlo](../TwoLevel/Mahlo.agda), [TwoLevel/Mahlo/StepAlgebra](../TwoLevel/Mahlo/StepAlgebra.agda), [Mahlo/Heights/Regular](../Mahlo/Heights/Regular.agda). | Proof on paper, 2026-11-03. The cardinal logic of §6 (Lemmas 6.4 and 6.7) is checked against named hypotheses, in cubical Agda modules not included in this release; everything else is proved on paper. |
| [Universal induction–recursion in simplicial sets](UniversalIR.md) | Interprets the universal induction–recursive type of IR/Universal in simplicial sets, with outputs in any Kan complex: the codes are the initial algebra of the polynomial functor of descriptions, and one chain over the space of codes gives the type with its decoding and induction as equalities of maps, stable under pullback (Theorems U, U′). Dybjer–Setzer's own decoding and induction equations then hold as equalities of maps (Corollary V), the construction runs in presheaf toposes (Corollary W), and at the codes of `Ambient.description` it gives StrictMahlo.md's Theorem A (Corollary S). Modules: [IR/Universal](../IR/Universal/README.md), [IR/Universal/Induction](../IR/Universal/Induction.agda), [Mahlo/DS/Ambient](../Mahlo/DS/Ambient.agda). | Proof on paper, 2026-11-03. The Agda side (the universal type with its realized laws by refl, the comparison with ordinary arguments, Dybjer–Setzer's induction rule) is checked, with no extensionality, in IR/Universal. |
| [The strict Mahlo rule for the universe of displays in simplicial sets](StrictDisplayMahlo.md) | The hypothesis `StrictDisplayMahlo` of LCTT's display model, whose operators are an ambient operator with a fiber part over it, so that a child has fiber codes over its base codes. Running StrictMahlo.md's construction a second time, over the carrier of its Theorem A, gives the fiber codes with their reading, every fiber law an equality of maps (Theorem D), hence a global element of `StrictDisplayMahlo ℓ` whose base half is StrictMahlo.md's element of `Strict-Mahlo (Ambient.formers ℓ)` (Corollary D′). The one new fact about the base is that Theorem A's carrier is the disjoint union of its constructor tags (Lemma 1.1). Modules: [TwoLevel/Mahlo](../TwoLevel/Mahlo.agda) for the base half, [TwoLevel/Mahlo/Displayed](../TwoLevel/Mahlo/Displayed.agda) for the rule (`Strict-Display-Mahlo`); the display model is LCTT's, not yet released. | Proof on paper, 2026-11-03. No step is checked in Agda; its cardinal steps are those checked for StrictMahlo.md, not rechecked for this chain. Independently audited, with the audit's corrections applied. |
| [Indexed induction–recursion in simplicial sets](IndexedUniversalIR.md) | Builds the induction–recursive family of IR/Indexed, one for all schemes of indexed polynomial induction–recursion, in simplicial sets, with decoding and induction as equalities of maps, stable under pullback (Theorem P); the ordinary arguments over it are the native ones (Corollary O). StrictDisplayMahlo.md's Theorem D is its instance at the display fiber schemes (Corollary F), and in presheaf toposes the same family exists with every Kan condition dropped (Corollary Q). Modules: [IR/Indexed](../IR/Indexed/README.md), [IR/Polynomial](../IR/Polynomial.agda), [IR/Polynomial/Factorization](../IR/Polynomial/Factorization.agda), [IR/Description/Indexed](../IR/Description/Indexed.agda), [IR/Indexed/General](../IR/Indexed/General.agda), [IR/Indexed/General/Reducibility](../IR/Indexed/General/Reducibility.agda). | Proof on paper, 2026-11-03. The Agda side, with its realized laws by refl, is checked in IR/Indexed. Independently audited: Theorem P and Corollary O stand, and Corollary F is repaired; Corollary Q was checked step by step. |

## How the notes depend on each other

Simplicial.md gives the ambient Mahlo rule with its law `inner-β` an
identification. StrictMahlo.md gives the strict form, every law an
equality of maps, on a different carrier. Its §1 (sizes, Kapulkin and
Lumsdaine's facts K1–K6, the assumptions T1 and T2) is shared by the three
notes that follow it. UniversalIR.md runs StrictMahlo.md's construction
over the codes of descriptions in place of the operators, and contains its
Theorem A at the codes of `Ambient.description` (Corollary S).
StrictDisplayMahlo.md extends StrictMahlo.md: it runs the same
construction a second time, over Theorem A's carrier, for the fiber codes.
IndexedUniversalIR.md is the indexed counterpart of UniversalIR.md, and
StrictDisplayMahlo.md's Theorem D is its instance at the display fiber
schemes (Corollary F).
