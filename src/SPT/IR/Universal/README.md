# Universal induction–recursion

One induction–recursive type for all Dybjer–Setzer descriptions of [IR/Description](../Description.agda). The universal type `U γ` and its decoding `value γ` are defined together. Its constructor takes an argument datatype `Args ε X v` with the subcode ε as a parameter, and the body of that datatype (`Body`) follows ε, so safe Agda accepts the recursion through the children at a variable code.

This is native induction–recursion, Agda's mutual datatype and function. It does not reduce induction–recursion to anything weaker; for that, see the generated parts of [IR](../README.md) and the equivalence of the smallness principle with the Mahlo rule in [Mahlo/DS](../../Mahlo/DS/README.md). Its realized laws compute at a variable code, and Dybjer–Setzer's decoding equation and induction rule follow from it with no extensionality. With induction on source arguments it is an algebra of its description to which IR's initiality theorems apply: under extensionality for functions from types at the arities' level, it is initial among the lawful algebras, and translations of descriptions act on universal types functorially. It is the type that the draft paper proof [Universal induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/UniversalIR.md) interprets in simplicial sets.

## The universal type and Dybjer–Setzer's rules

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Universal](../Universal.agda) | The type, its arguments and its decoding (`U`, in `Set s` for arities of level s; `Args`, `Body`, `value`, `out`). The realized laws hold by refl at every code: decoding (`output-β`), induction with the collected calls (`Method`, `ind`, `calls`, `ind-β`), root recovery (`root`, `root-β`). The comparison with ordinary arguments (`Shape`, with output `Mark`) uses no extensionality: both inverse identities (`to`, `from`, `from-to`, `to-from`), the outputs (`output-to`), the hypothesis types (`Hyp`, `Std-Hyp`, `hyp-to`) and the recursive calls (`std-calls`, `calls-to`). At δ both sides keep the complete child map. The argument constructors compute at an open continuation (`nil`, `sig`, `del`; `out-δ`, `hyp-δ`, `calls-δ`). Root recovery returns the child map itself (`selected`, `retains`). |
| [Induction](Induction.agda) | Dybjer–Setzer's induction rule, from the native one, with no extensionality. Introduction and methods on ordinary arguments (`intro`, `Method`). The argument comparison is an isomorphism (`arguments`), and its half-adjoint coherence carries a method to a native one, transporting the whole dependent method type at once (`Fiber`, `prepared`, `method`, `method-β`). The computation equation holds at every description, motive, method and argument (`ind`, `ind-β`). Induction recovers the ordinary argument of every element with its identification (`Source`, `recover`, `recover-β`), so introduction has a section (`root`, `root-β`, `section`). |
| [Discriminator](Discriminator.agda) | The universal type keeps whole child maps. For the description of a leaf or a binary node, with constant output (`γ`, `leaf`, `node`), two different child maps have decodings equal as whole functions (`outputs`, by refl), yet the maps and their parent nodes are distinct (`maps-distinct`, `nodes-distinct`). Root recovery returns the original child map by refl (`recover`, `retains`), and native induction sees the depths (`depth`, `plain-depth`, `nested-depth`, `parent-depth`). Dybjer–Setzer induction builds a family indexed by the actual tree, whose computation equation keeps the literal child map (`Trace`, `trace`, `leaf-β`, `node-β`). |

## Initiality of native induction

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Derivation](Derivation.agda) | The derivations of the elements, as closed trees of one signature whose judgments are the elements, one operation at each (`signature`). The positions of an element are those of its root, a point of the arity of one of the root's δ nodes, and the child at a position is the input there (`positions`, `child`). The element stays the judgment, so no element is rebuilt from a tree, and the signature lives at the level of `U γ` whatever the level of the decodings. Native hypotheses are read at the positions and tabulated from them (`hypothesis-at`, `tabulate`), and every element has its derivation, by the native induction (`derivation`). |
| [Initiality](Initiality.agda) | Pointwise initiality of native induction, with no extensionality. The hypotheses a valuation gives at an argument, at δ the function of its values on the child map (`values`), and the lifting of a relation between two motives to hypotheses (`Lifted`); at an induction its values and its calls agree at every child (`values-to-calls`, `calls-agree`). Relational uniqueness under the lax law (`Lax`, `ind-unique-rel`) is [Foundation/Tree/Fold/Initiality](../../Foundation/Tree/Fold/Initiality.agda)'s `induction-unique-rel` at the derivations of the elements ([Derivation](Derivation.agda)), for the relation of the valuation to induction (`Relates`): at a node the relatedness at the positions is the lifted relation (`related-hypotheses`), where the law applies (`related-node`). Its instances: the fundamental theorem (`Relational`, `ind-rel`); uniqueness for congruent methods, with induction satisfying their equations (`Identical`, `Congruent`, `Homomorphic`, `ind-unique`, `ind-homomorphic`); fusion along a map of motives (`mapped`, `Graph`, `graph-agreement`, `Carries`, `ind-fusion`). At δ the hypotheses are one function on the child map, so under extensionality for families over the arities every method is congruent (`agreement`, `congruent`). |
| [Initiality/Discriminator](Initiality/Discriminator.agda) | Initiality at Discriminator's leaves and binary nodes, whose hypotheses at a node are one function on the two children. Size reads it at both, so its methods are congruent with no extensionality, and every valuation with size's equations is size (`size-step`, `size`, `size-congruent`, `size-equations`, `size-unique`; `size-node` by refl). The fundamental theorem at the strict order, which is not reflexive: depth, reading the child ₀, lies below size (`Below`, `left-summand-below`, `related`, `depth-below-size`). Fusion carries depth along the successor (`successor`, `shifted`, `carries`, `successor-fusion`). |

## Source induction, algebras and translations

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Source](Source.agda) | Induction on source arguments, as [Base/Induction](../../Base/Induction.agda) specifies it at the source arguments of its description, with hypotheses as functions on positions. The universal type is an algebra of its description with its law, its operation Dybjer–Setzer's introduction and its law `output-to` (`algebra`, `law`). Tuple hypotheses are read at positions (`positioned`), and the recursive calls of a function so read are that function at the inputs, by refl at each δ, with no extensionality (`positioned-calls`). Dybjer–Setzer's rule gives source induction (`method`, `source`), with its computation identity under extensionality for functions on positions (`source-β`). Under the same extensionality the sections of every step form a contractible type (`sections-are-contractible`, by [Foundation/Induction/Initiality](../../Foundation/Induction/Initiality.agda)). |
| [Source/Discriminator](Source/Discriminator.agda) | Source induction keeps the children of a node apart. On Discriminator's description, the node whose child ₀ is a node and whose child ₁ a leaf (`lopsided`). A step reading the position of child ₀ counts two there, and one reading child ₁ counts one, by refl (`left`, `left-depth`, `right`, `right-depth`). Reading the tuple hypotheses with the children exchanged is a well-typed conversion at a constant motive, and it fails the law of `positioned-calls` (`exchanged`, `exchanged-calls-fail`). |
| [Morphism](Morphism.agda) | Initiality among the lawful algebras, from [IR/Morphism/Initiality](../Morphism/Initiality.agda) at source induction, under extensionality for functions from types at the arities' level: the fold into every lawful algebra is a morphism (`fold`), every morphism is the fold pointwise (`unique`), and the morphisms form a contractible type (`initial`). |
| [Morphism/Discriminator](Morphism/Discriminator.agda) | The fold out of the universal type computes, and the morphism laws exclude maps into the fibers. On Discriminator's description, the numbers with 0 at a leaf and a node one above its child ₀ form a lawful algebra (`depth-step`, `depths`, `depths-law`). Its fold counts two at Source/Discriminator's lopsided node, by refl at variable extensionality (`fold-depth`); the map that is 0 everywhere is not a morphism (`zero-not-morphism`). |
| [Translation](Translation.agda) | The action of translations ([IR/Translation/Morphism](../Translation/Morphism.agda)), as a fold, with its functor laws. Along a translation τ from γ to γ′, the universal type of γ with its source induction has a morphism into every lawful algebra of γ′ (`fold`); every morphism along τ is the fold pointwise (`unique`), and the morphisms form a contractible type (`initial`). The fold along the identity translation is the identity (`fold-identity`), and the fold along a composite is the composite of the folds, through the universal type of the middle description (`fold-compose`). All of these are [IR/Translation/Fold](../Translation/Fold.agda)'s at the universal type, under extensionality for functions from types at the arities' level. |
| [Translation/Discriminator](Translation/Discriminator.agda) | The action of a translation on the universal type is not the identity. On Discriminator's description, the translation exchanging the two children of every node (`other`, `mirror`) acts on the universal type by its fold into the universal type itself (`mirrored`). The lopsided node has depth two along child ₀, its image depth one, and its image along the composite of the exchange with itself depth two again, by refl at variable extensionality (`original-depth`, `mirrored-depth`, `twice-mirrored-depth`). So the fold along the exchange moves a tree (`mirror-moves`), where the fold along the identity translation keeps every tree (Translation's `fold-identity`). |

## What computes, and what is an identity

- **By refl, at a variable code:**
  - `value γ (intro a) ＝ out γ γ a`;
  - native `ind-β` and `root-β`;
  - `out-δ`, `hyp-δ` and `calls-δ` at an open continuation.
- **Identities, by induction on the code, with no extensionality:**
  - Dybjer–Setzer's decoding equation for ordinary arguments, `output-β` followed by `output-to`;
  - the hypothesis and call comparisons;
  - Dybjer–Setzer's induction equation (`Induction.ind-β`).

  At δ both presentations keep the complete child map f, so no equality of functions obtained from pointwise identities is needed.
- **Pointwise initiality, with no extensionality** (Initiality): relational uniqueness under the lax law, the fundamental theorem, and uniqueness and fusion for congruent methods. At δ the hypotheses are one function on the child map, so extensionality for families over the arities makes every method congruent.
- **Identities under extensionality for functions from types of level s, the arities' level** (Source, Morphism, Translation): the computation identity of source induction, and initiality among algebras and along translations with the functor laws. Source induction itself uses no extensionality, so its values compute at closed trees. A fold's step assembles the continuation after a δ at the identification of decodings that extensionality gives; where that continuation is the unit shape, as in the discriminators, the codes of the fold compute by refl.

## The model

The draft paper proof [Universal induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/UniversalIR.md) interprets the universal type in simplicial sets, for a strongly Mahlo cardinal (its Theorem U), so that, read in the model, the realized laws `output-β`, `ind-β` and `root-β` are equalities of maps. It then argues that Dybjer–Setzer's own rules hold there as equalities too (its Corollary V): the comparison and computation witnesses listed in its Lemma 7.1 are interpreted by reflexivity. The model is not formalized here.

## Not here

- No claim that the universal type comes from a weaker principle. That is the work of the generated parts of [IR](../README.md) and of [Mahlo/DS](../../Mahlo/DS/README.md), where the decoding equation holds as an identity at a variable description.
- The functor laws of the action of translations with no extensionality. Translation's `fold-identity` and `fold-compose` are identities under extensionality.
- Fusion out of the universal type. IR proves it for any algebra with source induction ([Morphism/Fusion](../Morphism/Fusion.agda)) and along translations for generated parts ([Translation/Fusion](../Translation/Fusion.agda)); neither is instantiated here.
- A comparison with the generated part of an algebra ([IR/Generated](../Generated.agda)), such as the raised fixed point of the identity algebra.
