# Universal indexed induction–recursion

One induction–recursive family for all schemes of indexed polynomial induction–recursion, over the codes of [IR/Polynomial](../Polynomial.agda). The carriers `U s i` and their readouts `value s i` are defined together with the arguments of every code over them (`Args`) and their information (`info`).

This is native induction–recursion, Agda's mutual datatype and functions. It does not reduce induction–recursion to anything weaker; for that, see the generated parts of [IR's polynomial codes](../README.md#indexed-polynomial-inductionrecursion), whose rules [Mahlo/DS](../../Mahlo/DS/README.md) derives from the smallness principle. Its realized laws compute at a variable scheme and code with no extensionality. It is the indexed counterpart of [IR/Universal](../Universal/README.md), and the family that the draft paper proof [Indexed induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/IndexedUniversalIR.md) builds in simplicial sets.

## What computes, and what is an identity

- **By refl, at a variable scheme, code and argument:**
  - `value s i (intro a) ＝ output (rule i) (info s (shape (rule i)) a)`;
  - `info-sigma`, `info-pi`;
  - native `ind-β`, `root-β` and `section`.
- **Pointwise initiality** of native induction (Initiality) and of Dybjer–Setzer's rule (General/Initiality) with no extensionality, and of ordinary induction (Induction/Initiality) with none beyond the comparison's: relational uniqueness under the lax law and the fundamental theorem; uniqueness and fusion for congruent methods. For the rule and for ordinary induction, the law is the native law at the images of shapes or of ordinary arguments, so it holds of the transferred method at every native argument; at a congruent native method, ordinary induction is native induction (`agrees-with-native`). Extensionality for the arities at the motive's level makes every method congruent.
- **Identities, under extensionality for families of information over the arities** (IR/Polynomial's `Extensionality`):
  - the comparison (`kept`, `from-info`) and `make-β`;
  - the ordinary `root-β` and `section`;
  - `method-β` and the ordinary `ind-β`.
- **Identities built from refl by induction on the code, with no extensionality** (General): Dybjer–Setzer's decoding equation for general codes, the argument comparison and isomorphism, and the computation equation of their elimination rule. At closed codes they compute (General/Discriminator). A δ becomes a product of inputs only, where the native and the ordinary informations agree by computation.

At a product the native and the ordinary informations are separate recursions, equal pointwise under one binder. Identifying them needs extensionality, and so does every continuation of a sum read after such a product. That is where these codes differ from IR/Universal's Dybjer–Setzer codes, whose δ keeps the whole child map on both sides and needs no extensionality.

## Representation

The arguments stay ordinary: Σ at a sum, Π at a product, and the child itself at an input. So the comparison is the identity at inputs and constants, and hypotheses compute at inputs without matching on the argument. One datatype over the roots (`Node`, at indices and at codes) carries the recursion: `traverse` wraps each part again, and `value` passes its own node on unchanged. Safe Agda accepts this recursion at a variable code; IR/Universal uses another form that it accepts, an argument datatype with the code as a parameter.

## The model

The draft paper proof [Indexed induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/IndexedUniversalIR.md) builds the universal family in simplicial sets, for a strongly Mahlo cardinal (its Theorem P, the indexed counterpart of Theorem U of [Universal induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/UniversalIR.md)), with decoding, induction and root recovery as equalities of maps. Its Corollary F derives Theorem D of [The strict Mahlo rule for the universe of displays in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/StrictDisplayMahlo.md), the fiber codes of the display model of LCTT, as the instance at one family of schemes, under the assumptions stated there.

IR/Indexed is read there through T1, the assumed reading of Agda in a model of two-level type theory in simplicial sets stated in [The strict ambient Mahlo rule in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/StrictMahlo.md), extended in two ways:
- to inner inductive families whose constructors target a variable index;
- to Agda's mutual definitions, as the reading of IR/Universal does.

IR/Polynomial's codes are such a family. Their information index is a variable, with an identification of it with the information the former computes, so the model reads them as a fibration over the information (§1.4 there). The codes written here are the refl ones, the pattern synonyms `var`, `con`, `sigma` and `pi`. The model is not formalized here.

## Not here

- **The comparison by reflexivity in the model.** The comparison's witnesses use extensionality, so the argument of Lemma 7.1 of [Universal induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/UniversalIR.md) (its Corollary V) does not apply as stated. In the model the two argument families coincide (Corollary O of [Indexed induction–recursion in simplicial sets](../../--drafts--/IndexedUniversalIR.md)), but the Agda terms of Comparison and Induction are not claimed to be read by reflexivity. For Dybjer–Setzer's general codes the comparison (General) uses no extensionality.
- **Kubánek's own motive.** General/Reducibility and its Kripke form eliminate into any motive over `Map`. His reads the syntactic eliminator maElim of the object theory, which is object syntax and not a parameter there.
- **A normalization model.** Kripke/Neutral reads back over abstract stages and syntax, with Kubánek's outer readback, normal forms and context extension as hypotheses (`Readback`, `Extension`). A Kripke model of the object theory's syntax (normal and neutral expressions, stages of contexts with typed renamings, the fundamental lemma) and normalization from it are not built here.
- **Contractible sections.** For Dybjer–Setzer's source induction, [Foundation/Induction/Initiality](../../Foundation/Induction/Initiality.agda) proves that the valuations with a step's computation equations form a contractible type. Here, for native and ordinary induction and for Dybjer–Setzer's general rule, only the pointwise form is proved.
- **Hypotheses recomputed on the ordinary argument.** Comparing them with the native ones at products would need extensionality at the level of the motive.
