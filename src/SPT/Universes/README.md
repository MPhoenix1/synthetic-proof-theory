# Universes

This folder holds one module, [ChildCollection](ChildCollection.agda): the rules Strong Collection and Smallification, read in the children of a Mahlo reflector. LCTT (Large Cardinal Type Theory, not part of this release) states these rules between a stratum and the one above; here the child takes the place of the lower stratum, and the parent's codes the place of the types one stratum up. Reflectors, closures and their children are those of [Mahlo/Specification](../Mahlo/Specification.agda), and decoding up to retraction is that of [Mahlo/Retraction](../Mahlo/Retraction.agda). The module is `--safe --without-K` and is written on SPT's base.

## Strong Collection and Smallification

| Module | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [ChildCollection](ChildCollection.agda) | In one child L of a reflection, the owners are the points of a child type, witnesses and evidence are parent codes, and coverage is observed through propositions of the decoding level ([Foundation/Covers](../Foundation/Covers.agda)). A refinement is a child code of actual occurrences, still covering every owner (the record `Refinement`), and a support is a child code of actual points of a covered parent type (the record `Small-Support`): Strong-Collection L = Π j Y H. Total j Y H → Refinement j Y H, and Smallification L = Π (a : C). Covered ℓ (D a) → Small-Support a. Given a point of the decoding of the parent's unit code, Strong Collection gives Smallification (`smallify`); with the parent's decoding up to retraction, Smallification gives Strong Collection (`strength`): smallify the parent's Σ of the witnesses and evidence over each owner, and collect with the child's Σ of the owners and their supports. Weak resizing into the child, the child's decoding classifying the parent's (`Classifies` of [Foundation/Impredicative/Classification](../Foundation/Impredicative/Classification.agda)), gives Smallification (`weak-to-smallification`) and, with decoding up to retraction, Strong Collection (`weak-to-collection`). With the refinement covered rather than given (`Covered-Collection`, which Strong Collection gives by `covering`), Strong Collection is the form a classical reading in cubical Agda can keep; Smallification with the support covered (`Covered-Smallification`) holds given a point of the decoding of the parent's unit code (`covered-smallification`). For a reflector M, the rules ask these of every child M returns (`Collection-Rule`, `Smallification-Rule`, `Covered-Collection-Rule`, and `Resizing-Rule`, a weak presentation of every parent code in every child), as the degrees of [Mahlo/Degree](../Mahlo/Degree.agda) ask a reflector of each child; the implications between the rules are `smallify-rule`, `strength-rule`, `resizing-to-collection` and `covering-rule`. |
